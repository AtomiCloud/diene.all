---
name: vision-loop
description: Use to review Flutter UI visually across representative sizes, locales, and themes without golden baselines.
---

Read [the frontend UX standard](../../../docs/standards/frontend-ux/index.md).
Capture emulator screenshots across representative sizes and both themes,
inspect them with vision or a human, fix visible defects, and repeat. Never add
pixel-golden or screenshot snapshot gates.
